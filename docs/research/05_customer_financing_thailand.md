## In-House Dealer Financing

**Concept:** We lend money to customer directly from our balance sheet

**Pros:**
- Control entire process
- Keep all interest (instead of bank commission)
- Faster approval (no bank underwriting)

**Cons:**
- Tie up capital (需 prepay suppliers)
- Credit risk (defaults)
- Need money lending license? (Possibly — check)
- Collection overhead

**Example:**
- System price: ฿350,000
- Down payment: 10% (฿35,000)
- Financed: ฿315,000 over 5 years (60 months)
- Interest: 0% (0% promo) or 3% (if we need margin)
- Monthly payment: ฿5,250 (@ 0%) or ฿5,587 (@3%)
- Our capital requirement: ฿315,000 x number of financed deals

**ROI:** If we finance 10 deals simultaneously: ฿3.15M tied up. Is that acceptable?

## PPA (Power Purchase Agreement) Model

**We own the system, customer pays for energy consumed.**

**Use case:** Commercial customers (businesses) who want zero CAPEX

**Structure:**
- We install system on customer's roof (ours)
- Customer signs 10-25 year PPA
- Monthly bill based on kWh produced ( cheaper than grid )
- Rate: ฿3-4/kWh (vs grid ฿5)

**Revenue stream:**
- Stable monthly cash flow (like subscription)
- Long-term contract (10-25 years)
-Ownership transfer at end? (or renew)

**Challenges:**
- Requires significant capital (we fund all installations)
- Credit risk on customer (business might fail)
- Property rights (what if customer sells building?)

**Popular with:** Factories, warehouses, large commercial rooftops.

## Financing Decision Matrix

| Option | Our Risk | Customer Ease | Our Revenue | Complexity |
|--------|----------|---------------|-------------|------------|
| **Bank loan** | Low (bank underwrites) | Medium (must qualify) | Commission 1-2% | Low |
| **Leasing** | Medium (credit risk) | High (easier approval) | Lease margin 5-10% | Medium |
| **In-house 0%** | High (我们 вкладываем) | Very high | No interest, but full margin | High |
| **PPA** | Very high (own asset, credit) | Highest (no upfront) | Long-term stable | Very high |

**Recommendation for startup:**
- Start with **bank partnerships** (low risk, commission-based)
- Add **leasing** later when have capital
- Avoid **in-house financing** until have large cash reserves
- Consider **PPA** for commercial >50kW with strong tenant
