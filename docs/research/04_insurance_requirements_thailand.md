## Executive Summary

**Types of insurance needed:**
1. **Public Liability** — third party during installation (injury, property damage)
2. **Workman's Compensation** — mandatory for employees
3. **Contractor's All-Risk** — equipment, materials during installation

5. **Warranty Insurance** — performance guarantee (optional for customer)

- Tawan Power (company) → needs Public Liability + Workman's Comp + CAR
- Customer (homeowner) → may want extended warranty insurance
- Installers (if contractors) → should have their own liability (we verify)

## Detailed Findings (TODO)

### 1. Public Liability Insurance

**Purpose:** Covers third-party claims for:
- Injury to person during installation (e.g., installer drops panel on visitor)
- Damage to customer's property (e.g., roof damage, water leak)
- Damage to neighboring properties (e.g., panel falls to neighbor's yard)

**Coverage limits:**
- Minimum: ฿1,000,000 (1M)
- Recommended: ฿5,000,000-10,000,000 (5-10M)
- Premium: ~฿10,000-30,000/year depending on limit, turnover

**Requirements:**
- Insurer must be licensed in Thailand
- Certificate of Insurance (COI) for each project (customer may request)
- COI should name customer as "additional insured"
- Policy must include "sudden and accidental" coverage

---

### 2. Workman's Compensation Insurance (ประกัน scholarium)

**Legal requirement:** Mandatory for all employees under Thai law

**Coverage:**
- Work-related injury or illness
- Medical expenses, lost wages, disability, death benefits
- Covers both onsite and travel

**Who needs it:** All employees of Tawan Power (full-time, part-time) + possibly contractors if we control their work? ( safer to require contractors to have own insurance )

**Cost:** ~0.5-1% of payroll
- Example: payroll ฿1M/year → premium ฿5,000-10,000/year

**How to get:**
- Purchase from licensed insurer (FWD, Tokio Marine, Bangkok Insurance)
- Must register with Social Security Office
- Premium paid monthly or annually

**Penalty for non-compliance:** Fine, business shutdown, personal liability

---

### 3. Contractor's All-Risk (CAR) Insurance

**Purpose:** Covers physical loss/damage to:
- Materials in transit (panels, inverter shipped to site)
- Materials stored on site before installation
- Equipment during installation (tools, scaffolding)
- Installation itself (while work in progress)

**Coverage period:** From departure from warehouse to handover to customer

**Limits:** Usually based on project value (e.g., ฿500,000 per project)

**Cost:** ~0.5-1% of project value
- Example: ฿350,000 project → premium ฿1,750-3,500 per project

**When needed:** For each installation project (can be per-project policy)

---

### 4. Professional Indemnity (Errors & Omissions)

**Purpose:** Covers financial loss from:
- Design errors (miscalculated system size)
- Specification errors
- Negligence in professional services

**When needed:** If customer sues because system underperforms due to design flaw

**Limits:** ฿1M-5M
**Premium:** ~฿5,000-15,000/year

**Not strictly required** but recommended for consulting services

---

### 5. Warranty Insurance (for customers)

**Optional add-on:** Sell to customers as extended warranty

**What it covers:** Beyond manufacturer warranty:
- Performance guarantee (system produces X% of expected)
- Labor warranty (5-10 years on workmanship)
- Replacement of defective parts (even if manufacturer denies claim)

**Revenue:** Can charge ฿10,000-30,000 extra per system
**Cost to us:** reinsurance premium pass-through

## ❓ Open Questions

1. **Do subcontractors need their own insurance?** We should request COI from each subcontractor (installer partners)
2. **Can we bundle all insurance with one provider?** Yes, often cheaper
3. **Does manufacturer warranty require certified installer?** Often yes — we need certification from Jinko/Huawei
4. **What about equipment while in storage?** Need separate "property insurance" for warehouse
5. **Claim process:** Who handles, timeline?
6. **Deductible:** What's the excess on each policy? (฿5,000-10,000 typical)
7. **Does customer need separate homeowners insurance endorsement?** Recommend they check with insurer

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Get Public Liability ฿5M minimum** —protects against catastrophic claims
2. **Register for Workman's Comp immediately** upon hiring first employee (mandatory, fines for non-compliance)
3. **Use Contractor's All-Risk per project** — pass cost to customer as "insurance fee"
4. **Require subcontractors to show COI** before allowing them on site
5. **Keep all insurance certificates on file** (digital copies)
6. **Annual review:** increase limits as revenue grows
7. **Safety first:** insurance is backup, but prevention better (training, safety gear, checklists)
