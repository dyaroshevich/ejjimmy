## TISI Certification

**TISI** = Thai Industrial Standards Institute

**Which products need TISI?**
- Solar panels: mandatory? ( unclear — many imports have no TISI but sold )
- Inverters: mandatory? (probably yes for grid-tied)
- Batteries: maybe not

**Reality:** Most imported solar equipment sold in Thailand WITHOUT TISI certificate. Customs may allow if has international certs (IEC, UL). But utilities (MEA/PEA) may require TISI for interconnection?

**Action:** Confirm with MEA if TISI required for inverter. If yes, must buy TISI-approved models (Huawei, Growatt probably have).

## Electrical Contractor License

**Authority:** Ministry of Industry (กรม emanations)

**Requirements:**
- Company must have licensed electrician on staff
- Application for Electrical Contractor License
- Minimum: 1 licensed electrician supervisor
- Renewal annually

**Process:**
- Submit application with company documents
- Show proof of qualified electrician ( certificates )
- Pay fee (~฿5,000)
- Inspection possible

**Why needed:** Required for electrical work permit from MEA/PEA

## Standards Compliance

**International standards adopted in Thailand:**
- **IEC 61730** — photovoltaic module safety
- **IEC 62446** — PV system — requirements for documentation, commissioning, inspection
- **IEC 62116** — anti-islanding test for inverters
- **UL 1741** — similar to IEC but US

**What to check in equipment:**
- Inverter must have "anti-islanding protection" ( automatically disconnect from grid if grid fails )
- Must meet Thai voltage/frequency standards (220V ±10%, 50Hz)
- Must have built-in RCD (residual current device)
