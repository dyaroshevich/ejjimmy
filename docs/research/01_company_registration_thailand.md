## Detailed Findings

### 1. Company Structures Available to Foreigners

#### 1.1 Sole Proprietorship
- **Foreign ownership:** Not allowed (must be Thai national)
- **Liability:** Unlimited (personal assets at risk)
- **Not suitable** for our case

#### 1.2 Partnership
- **Types:** Ordinary partnership, limited partnership
- **Foreign ownership:** Restricted, needs Foreign Business License
- **Liability:** Partners liable (unlimited for general partners)
- **Not recommended** — too much personal risk

#### 1.3 Private Limited Company (Ltd.)
- **Thai name:**บริษัท จำกัด (มหาชน) для public, ส微小 for private
- **Foreign ownership:** Up to 49% без BOI, 100% с BOI approval
- **Liability:** Limited to share capital
- **Minimum capital:** ฿100,000 (но банки требуют ฿1M для opening account)
- **Shareholders:** Min 2 persons (can be individuals or companies)
- **Directors:** Min 1 (can be foreign)
- ** Auditor:** Required if turnover >฿10M/year
- **Timeline:** 1-2 недели для регистрации
- **Cost:** Government fees ~฿5,000, lawyer fees ~฿20,000-50,000

**Verdict:** Viable option if we accept 49% foreign ownership (with Thai nominee shareholders — risky) или apply for BOI promotion.

#### 1.4 Public Limited Company
- **Too early** for us (requires min 2 shareholders, min 5M shares, listed?)
- **Skip**

#### 1.5 BOI-Promoted Company
- **Защита:** Board of Investment promotion
- **Foreign ownership:** 100% allowed (foreign business license exempt)
- **Tax incentives:** 长达8年的公司税收豁免（通常20%的企业所得税） + 进口设备免税 + 土地所有权（外国人可持有）
- **Минимальный капитал:** ฿1M (всего, не-obligatory но рекомендуется)
- **Требования:**
  - Проект должен соответствовать "Priorities" (технологии, инновации, солнечная энергия — likely qualifies)
  - Минимум 10泰国员工 в течение 2 лет (или ฿1M payroll?)
  - Инвестиции в оборудование: минимум ฿1M?
  - Технологический трансфер (обучение泰国 сотрудников)
- **Срок действия:** 8 years tax exemption + 5 years 50% reduction afterwards
- **Application timeline:** 3-6 месяцев
- **Cost:** BOI application fee ~฿50,000-100,000 (через lawyer)

**Verdict:** **Best option** для нас — получаем 100% foreign ownership, tax-free для первых 8 лет.

### 2. Foreign Business License (если без BOI)

**Если не пойдем via BOI,** нужно Foreign Business License (FBL) для industries, which are "restricted".

**List of Restricted Businesses (зарезервированы для泰国цев):**
1. Media (newspaper, radio, TV)
2. Telecommunication
3. Aviation
4. Land trading
5. Mining
6. **Is solar installation restricted?** Likely NOT — это "services" or "trading" maybe allowed

**Procedure:**
- Apply to Ministry of Commerce
- Show that business does not fall under restricted list
- If denied, can appeal
- Takes ~3 months

**Verdict:** Since solar probably not restricted, we could register Ltd without BOI but limited to 49% foreign. Use Thai nominees (highly risky) или BOI. **BOI preferred**.

### 3. BOI Promotion Deep Dive

#### 3.1 Eligibility Criteria for Solar/Energy Business

**BOI Priority Activities (2026):**
- Alternative energy (including solar)
- Smart grid technology
- Energy storage
- High-tech manufacturing
- Digital services

**Our business model:**
- Solar system design and installation → qualifies under "alternative energy services"
- AI-powered chat → qualifies under "digital services"
- Combined: strong case

**Requirements:**
1. **Minimum investment:** ฿1M in machinery/equipment (excluding land, working capital)
2. **Minimum capital:** ฿1M registered capital (не обязательно вкладывать, но указать)
3. **Employment:** At least 10 Thai employees OR payroll of ฿1M/month within 2 years
4. **Technology:** Use of advanced technology (AI qualifies?) + training provided to Thais
5. **Location:** Can be anywhere (Bangkok, Phuket, Chiang Mai) — no restrictions
6. **Land:** With BOI, foreigners can own land (but must be用于 business, not residential)

#### 3.2 Tax Incentives Breakdown

| Incentive | Details | Value |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| **Corporate income tax exemption** | 8 years from first profit | 0% vs 20% → save ~฿400,000/year на прибыль ฿2M |
| **Import duty exemption** | Machinery, equipment, raw materials | Up to 100% depending on item |
| **Double deduction** | 5 years: transportation, electricity, water, training costs | 200% deduction → reduces taxable income |
| **Land ownership** | Foreign company can own land | Priceless for building warehouse/office |
| **Duty-free raw materials** | For export-oriented projects | Not relevant (we serve local) |
| **Fast-track visa** | For foreign executives (1 year) | Convenience |

**Caveats:**
- Must maintain BOI conditions (investment, employment)
- Annual report to BOI
- Cannot engage in restricted businesses
- Tax holiday applies to net profit, not revenue

#### 3.3 BOI Application Process

```
Step 1: Prepare application dossier (4-6 недель)
├── Business plan (detailed: market, operations, financial projections 3 years)
├── Company affidavit (一旦公司注册后)
├── Evidence of funds (bank statement showing ฿1M capital)
├── Investment plan (equipment list, cost)
├── Employment plan (number of Thai staff, training)
├── Location details (property lease/purchase agreement)
└── Technology description (how we use AI, advanced tech)

Step 2: Submit to BOI office (Bangkok)
├── In person or through lawyer
├── Interview with BOI officer (explain project)
├── Q&A session (1-2 hours)
└── Pay application fee (฿50,000-100,000)

Step 3: BOI review (2-4 недели)
├── They assess: technology level, benefit to Thailand, compliance
├── May request additional documents
├── Site visit possible
└── Decision: Approved / Conditional / Rejected

Step 4: BOI Certificate issued (1 неделя)
├── Certificate number
├── Validity: 8 years + 5 years half-tax
└── Conditions listed

Total timeline: 2-3 месяца (сэкономьте на lawyer)
```

**Cost breakdown:**
- Lawyer fees for BOI application: ฿100,000-300,000 (если делаем сами — дешевле но рискуем)
- Government fees: ฿50,000-100,000
- Company registration: ฿20,000
- **Total:** ฿170,000-420,000 (~$5,000-12,000)

### 4. Registration Process Step-by-Step

#### Phase A: Pre-Registration (Week 1-2)

1. **Company name reservation**
   - Submit 3 name options to DBD
   - Check availability (ตรวจสอบ)
   - Cost: ฿100-300
   - Timeline: 1-3 дня

2. **Memorandum of Association**
   - Drafted by lawyer
   - Contains: company name, purpose, capital, shareholders, directors
   - Register with DBD

3. **Registered capital deposit**
   - Open temporary bank account (escrow)
   - Deposit ฿100,000-1,000,000 (shows commitment)
   - Get bank letter confirming deposit

#### Phase B: Registration (Week 2-3)

4. **Submit registration to DBD**
   - Forms: Bor. Tor. 01, 02, 03
   - Attach: Memorandum, shareholder list, director list, registered capital proof
   - Fee: ฿5,000 ( tasa) + ฿100/page for gazette
   - Timeline: 5-7 working days

5. **Receive Certificate of Incorporation**
   - Документ: "ใบスポーツ证明书" (Certificate of Incorporation)
   - Company established!

6. **Tax ID registration**
   - Submit to Revenue Department
   - Get tax ID number (เลขประจำตัวประชาชน/091... для компании)
   - VAT registration если revenue >฿1.8M/year (можно добровольно)

#### Phase C: Post-Registration (Week 3-4)

7. **Open business bank account**
   - Use Certificate of Incorporation
   - Requires: directors present, IDs, proof of address
   - Banks: Bangkok Bank, Kasikorn, SCB, Krungsri
   - Minimum deposit: ฿10,000-100,000

8. **Register for Social Security**
   - When you hire first employee (включая себя если director)
   - At Social Security Office (สภaminimum)
   - Employer contribution: 5% of salary (max ฿750/month)
   - Employee contribution: 5%

9. **Work permit application for foreign employees** (если будет foreign director)
   - Non-Immigrant B visa first
   - Work permit application at Department of Employment
   - Requirements: company registered, capitalization proof, job description
   - Timeline: 2-3 недели
   - Cost: ฿2,000-3,000

10. **Annual compliance setup**
    - Accounting system (Xero/QuickBooks)
    - Appointment of auditor (если нужен)
    - Tax filing calendar

### 6. Thai Labor Requirements (if hiring)

**Social Security (สมทบ):**
- All employees (Thais and foreigners with work permit) must register
- Employer pays 5% of salary (max ฿750/month → ceiling ~฿15,000 salary)
- Employee pays 5% (matched)
- Covers: health insurance, retirement, disability

**Workman's Compensation (ประกัน Scholarium):**
- Mandatory for all employees
- Premium: 0.2-1% of payroll (depends on risk classification)
- Solar installation = high risk? Likely 0.8-1%
- Must purchase from approved insurer (FWD, Tokio Marine)

** severance Pay (บำนาญ):**
- Based on length of service:
  - <120 days: 7 days notice or pay in lieu
  - 120-365 days: 30 days
  - 1-3 years: 60 days
  - 3-6 years: 90 days
  - 6-10 years: 180 days
  - 10+ years: 300 days
- Paid at current salary rate

**Other benefits (customary):**
- 13th month bonus (通常December, 1 month salary)
- Annual salary increase (5-10% typical)
- Health insurance (optional but expected for office staff)
- Transportation allowance (฿1,000-2,000/month)
- Meal allowance (฿300-500/day)

## ✅ Recommendations

### What to DO:

1. **Apply for BOI promotion** — это самый чистый путь, дает 100% foreign ownership, tax-free 8 years
2. **Hire Thai lawyer** specializing BOI applications (например, Chandler & Thong-ek, or P&K Law) — стоит ฿100-300k но снижает риск отказа
3. **Prepare detailed business plan** emphasizing:
   - Advanced technology (AI chat, automation)
   - Training for Thai employees (knowledge transfer)
   - Contribution to Thailand's renewable energy goals
4. **Register company first (Private Ltd) while BOI application processing** — можно начать операционную деятельность раньше
5. **Budget:**
   - Company registration: ฿45,000
   - BOI application (lawyer + fees): ฿300,000
   - **Total:** ฿345,000 (~$10,000)
6. **Timeline:** 4 months total (1 month для компании, 3 месяца для BOI)

### What NOT to do:

1. **Don't use Thai nominee shareholders** — high risk of losing company ( nominee can claim ownership )
2. **Don't skip lawyer** — BOI application nuanced, one mistake = rejection
3. **Don't undercapitalize** — BOI looks at registered capital, ฿1M minimum recommended
4. **Don't start operations before company registration** —个人代言 = personal liability
5. **Don't ignore social security registration** — mandatory, penalties for non-compliance

### Timeline:

- **Week 1-2:** Retain lawyer, prepare documents (business plan, investment plan)
- **Week 3-4:** Name search, register company (Private Ltd) — this can happen in parallel
- **Week 5-8:** Submit BOI application
- **Week 9-12:** BOI review (follow up with lawyer)
- **Month 4:** BOI approval + certificate
