## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Start with Google Search + Facebook** (70/30 split)
2. **Create dedicated landing pages** (not home page) with clear CTA
3. **Track everything** with UTM parameters and GA4
4. **Test 3-4 ad creatives per channel** first week
5. **Pause low performers after 1 week** (CPC > target, CR < 2%)
6. **Implement retargeting** from day 1 (pixel on landing page)
7. **Build SEO foundation** now, reap benefits in 3-6 months
8. **Hire freelance Thai copywriter** for ad creatives (better than English direct translation)
9. **Set up call tracking** to know which ads generate phone calls
10. **Daily monitoring:** first week, then weekly optimization
