## Executive Summary

**Проблема:** После установки система должна работать 25 лет. Что если:
- Inverter сломается через 3 года?
- Panel упадет coefficient через 5?
- Customer недоволен performance?

**Решение:** Послепродажное обслуживание для:
- Удержания клиентов (retencion)
- Рефералов (счастливые клиенты приводят других)
- Доп. доход (maintenance contracts)
- Репутации (online reviews)

## Warranty Claims Process

### Manufacturer warranties:
- Panels: 12-25 years performance warranty (80% output after 25 years)
- Inverters: 5-10 years standard, extendable to 20-25
- Workmanship: our warranty 5 years

**Process:**
1. Customer reports issue (phone/email)
2. We diagnose (remote monitoring?)
3. Determine cause: equipment defect vs installation error
4. If equipment: file RMA with manufacturer
5. If workmanship: dispatch technician
6. Repair/replace
7. Follow-up to confirm resolution

**Timeline SLA:**
- Acknowledge receipt: within 4 hours
- Diagnosis: within 24 hours
- Resolution: within 7 days (parts may take time)

## Maintenance Contracts

**Annual maintenance fee:** ฿5,000-15,000/system

**What's included:**
- Annual inspection (1x per year)
- Panel cleaning (2x per year)
- Inverter diagnostics
- Performance report
- Priority service (if issue, jump queue)

**Uptake rate expectation:** 30-50% of customers

**Revenue:**
- 100 systems × ฿10,000 avg = ฿1M/year recurring

**Cost:**
- Technician time: ฿2,000/visit
- Cleaning supplies: ฿500
- Profit margin: ~70%

## Service Level Agreements (SLA)

### Response times:
- Critical (no power): 24 hours
- High (performance issue): 3 days
- Low (inquiry): 5 days

### Availability:
- Support hours: Mon-Fri 9-18, Sat 9-12
- After-hours emergency: on-call rotation (paid extra)

### Compensation for missed SLA:
- Credit 10% of annual maintenance fee
- Free service visit

## Customer Satisfaction Tracking

**Metrics:**
- NPS (Net Promoter Score): "Would you recommend us?"
- CSAT (Customer Satisfaction): rate 1-5 after each service
- Reviews: Google, Facebook

**How to improve:**
- Follow-up call after installation (day 3, day 30)
- Address complaints quickly
- Ask for reviews (incentivize with ฿500 discount on maintenance)

## ❓ Open Questions

1. What's typical warranty claim rate? (1-3% per year?)
2. How long does manufacturer take to approve RMA? (2-4 weeks)
3. Should we stock common spare parts? (inverters, optimizers)
4. Do customers expect free maintenance first year? (Yes, typical)
5. How to structure maintenance contract? (Annual auto-renewal?)
6. What coverage for weather damage? (Usually not covered — homeowner insurance)

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Offer 1-year free maintenance** with every installation (cost ฿5,000 but builds loyalty)
2. **Price annual maintenance at ฿10,000** (discount if paid upfront)
3. **Use simple tool** initially (Google Calendar + WhatsApp) — upgrade when >50 maintenance customers
4. **Create service menu** with fixed prices (easier to quote)
5. **Track all service calls** in CRM (time, parts, outcome)
6. **Train 1-2 technicians** dedicated to service (not installation crew)
7. **Set up automated reminders** for annual maintenance (6 months before expiry)
8. **Monitor reviews** daily — respond to negative within 24h
