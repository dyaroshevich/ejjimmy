## Executive Summary

**Проблема:** Старые солнечные панели (через 25+ лет) и инверторы становятся отходами электроники (e-waste). Требуется законное утилизацию.

**Regulations:** Thailand has strict e-waste laws — illegal dumping = heavy fines.

**Solution:** Partner with certified e-waste recycler, include recycling cost in pricing.

---

## E-Waste Regulations in Thailand

### What's regulated:
- Solar panels ( classified as hazardous waste? lead solder )
- Inverters ( electronic waste )
- Batteries ( lithium-ion — hazardous )
- Cables, metal racking ( maybe recyclable )

### Legal requirements:
- **Cannot dispose in landfill**
- Must use licensed e-waste recycler
- Document waste transfer (manifest)
- Pay recycling fee

---

## Panel End-of-Life

**Timeline:** Panels last 25-30 years. First replacements in ~2050. But some fail earlier (defects, damage).

**Recycling process:**
1. Remove panel from roof
2. Send to recycler
3. Recycler separates: glass (~70%), aluminum frame (~18%), silicon (~5%), metals (copper, silver)
4. Recovery rate: ~85-95% of materials

**Cost:** ฿100-300 per panel (recycling fee)

**Do manufacturers take back?** Some do (Jinko recycling program?) — research

## Battery Recycling

**If we sell battery storage:**
- Lithium-ion batteries = hazardous waste
- Special handling required
- Higher recycling cost: ฿500-1,000 per kWh
- Must be recycled at specialized facility

**Recommendation:** Include battery recycling fee in price, partner with recycler upfront.

## Financial Planning

### Include recycling fee in price:

| Item | Recycling cost (฿) | Markup | Customer fee |
|------|-------------------|--------|--------------|
| Panel (per Wp) | 0.10 | 100% | 0.20/Wp |
| Inverter (per kW) | 300 | 100% | 600/kW |
| Battery (per kWh) | 500 | 100% | 1,000/kWh |

**Example 10kW system:**
- 25 panels × 0.20/Wp × 400W = ฿2,000
- Inverter 10kW × 600 = ฿6,000
- Total recycling fund: ฿8,000 collected now, invested, used in 25 years

**Alternative:** Don't pre-collect — charge when recycling needed. But then customer may not pay.
