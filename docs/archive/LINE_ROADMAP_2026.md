# 🚀 LINE Marketing Funnel 2026: Solar Revenue Engine
**Project:** Tawan Power (Solar Krabi)
**Strategy:** Transforming LINE Official Account into a High-Conversion Sales Machine

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary
In 2026, a "Chatbot" is not enough. For Tawan Power, we are building a **Conversational Ecosystem**. By integrating the website's AI intelligence with LINE's powerful features (Messaging API, LIFF, Flex Messages), we create a seamless funnel that captures, qualifies, and converts leads automatically.

---

## 🏗️ The Funnel Architecture: "Trust-to-Contract"

### Phase 1: Interactive Awareness (The "Expert" Layer)
**Goal:** Establish authority and answer 90% of FAQ instantly.
*   **AI Knowledge Base (RAG):** Our AI Advisor, trained on `knowledge-base.json`, answers complex questions about ROI, Salt Corrosion in Krabi, and PEA regulations directly in the LINE chat.
*   **Rich Menu v1:** A 6-tile navigation system (Catalog, FAQ, Case Studies, Pricing, Contact Engineer).
*   **Instant Flex Responses:** Instead of plain text, the AI sends beautiful cards for product recommendations.

### Phase 2: Frictionless Conversion (The "Utility" Layer)
**Goal:** Turn curious followers into "Qualified Leads" without them ever leaving LINE.
*   **LIFF Solar Calculator:** A full-screen mini-app inside LINE where users calculate their savings in 60 seconds.
*   **Automatic Profile Capture:** We automatically get the user's LINE name and Profile Picture to personalize the proposal.
*   **Personalized Proposals:** Users receive a custom "Solar Summary" Flex Message in their chat history after using the calculator.

### Phase 3: Viral Expansion (The "Growth" Layer)
**Goal:** Leverage the user's social circle in Krabi to lower CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost).
*   **Viral Share Picker:** A "Share with Neighbor" button that uses LINE's Target Picker to send a recommendation card to friends/groups.
*   **Broadcast Automation:** Segmented broadcasts based on user interests (e.g., sending "Hotel Solar ROI" only to business leads).
*   **CRM Sync (Supabase):** The client (Admin) sees a full dashboard with LINE identities, calculation history, and "Probability of Deal" scores.

## 📈 Marketing Concepts for Presentation

### 1. The "Zero-Exit" Tunnel
Unlike a website where users can bounce to a competitor's tab, the **LINE Tunnel** keeps them within our branded environment. From the first question to the final survey booking, the user stays "inside" Tawan Power.

### 2. Social Proof & Trust
Seeing a professional "Solar Expert" in their familiar LINE interface creates 3x more trust than an anonymous web form. The ability to "Call Engineer" with one tap directly from the chat bridge the gap between digital and physical trust.

### 3. Data-Driven Sales
We provide the client not just with a phone number, but with a **Contextual Profile**:
> *"Khun Somchai from Ao Nang, monthly bill ฿5,500, interested in Jinko Panels, calculated 4.2 years payback."*
