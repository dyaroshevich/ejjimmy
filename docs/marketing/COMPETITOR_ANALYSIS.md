## 🏪 Major Competitors in Krabi

### 1. **Solaric (Solaric Co., Ltd.)**
- **Website:** solaric.co.th
- **Location:** Bangkok-based, serves Krabi (travel)
- **Services:** Installation only (no consultation)
- **Pricing:** Premium, ~฿35-45/W
- **Warranty:** 25-year panel, 10-year inverter
- **Reviews:** 4.5/5 on Google (200+ reviews)
- **Strengths:** Established brand, 10+ years, Thai-owned
- **Weaknesses:** Expensive, no AI, slow process
- **Our differentiation:** Lower cost (via efficiency), faster qualification via AI

---

### 2. **Power Solar ( powersolar.co.th )**
- **Location:** Phuket-based, serves Krabi
- **Services:** Design + installation
- **Pricing:** Mid-range ~฿30-38/W
- **Specialty:** Residential & commercial
- **Reviews:** 4.2/5 (100+ reviews)
- **Strengths:** Local Phuket presence, good customer service
- **Weaknesses:** No chatbot, phone-only contact

## 💰 Pricing Comparison (Cost per Watt)

Based on market research (May 2026):

| Tier | Price/watt (THB) | Installers | Target market |
|------|-----------------|------------|---------------|
| **Budget** | ฿20-28 | Green Solar, some locals | Price-sensitive, low quality |
| **Mid-range** | ฿28-35 | Power Solar, Solar1889, Andaman Solar | Mainstream |
| **Premium** | ฿35-45+ | Solaric, EA Solar, Solarsmart | High-end, large projects |

**Tawan Power positioning:** Mid-range with premium service (AI consultation, no-pressure). Target price: ฿30-38/W depending on system size and panel brand.

**Commission model:** We're not an installer, we're a lead generator. So our "revenue" is commission ~10% of deal value. Installers keep the rest.

## 🎯 Customer Journey Comparison

| Stage | Traditional Installer | Tawan Power |
|-------|----------------------|-------------|
| **Awareness** | Google search → ad → website → call | Google search → blog/article → AI chat instant |
| **Consideration** | Sales call/visit (high pressure) | AI chat (no pressure) + optional engineer call |

| **Decision** | Sales tactics, "limited time offer" | Customer decides at own pace, we follow up politely |
| **Handoff** | Direct sale | Lead passed to partner installer (we earn commission) |
| **After-sales** | Installer handles | Installer handles, we follow up for reviews |

**Our advantage:** Lower friction, faster, more educational, builds trust.

## 📈 SWOT Analysis

### Strengths
- ✅ AI-powered instant qualification (unique in Krabi)
- ✅ No-pressure sales approach (appeals to Western/expats)
- ✅ Content marketing (SEO) can drive organic leads
- ✅ Commission model: no inventory risk
- ✅ Scalable (one AI serves many)

### Weaknesses
- ❌ No installation capability (dependency on partners)
- ❌ No brand recognition yet
- ❌ Limited to lead generation, not full project margin
- ❌ Need to ensure partner quality (risk to reputation)

### Opportunities
- ✅ Expand to Phuket, Phang Nga, Surat Thani
- ✅ B2B white-label to other installers
- ✅ Add premium services (detailed reports, financing)
- ✅ Government incentives may increase solar adoption

### Threats
- ⚠️ Installers may start their own AI chatbots
- ⚠️ Price wars from cheap Chinese imports
- ⚠️ Regulatory changes (subsidy cuts, permitting)
- ⚠️ Economic downturn reduces discretionary spending
