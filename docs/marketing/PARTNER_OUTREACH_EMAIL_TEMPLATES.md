# 🤝 Partner Outreach Email Templates

**Purpose:** Templates for contacting solar installation companies in Krabi/Thailand to become partners  
**Goal:** Get them to agree to receive qualified leads from Tawan Power for commission  
**Audience:** Solar installation companies, electrical contractors, EPC firms  
**Language:** Thai (primary) + English (for international companies)

---

## 📧 Quick Pitch (Elevator Pitch)

**What we do:** Tawan Power is an AI-powered solar consultation platform that generates qualified leads from homeowners and businesses in Krabi. We provide free preliminary estimates, then connect customers with licensed installers for installation. We earn commission only when deal closes.

**Why partners should join:**
- **Free qualified leads** — no cost to join, pay only per closed installation
- **Pre-screened customers** — we've already qualified budget, intent, system size
- **Local focus** — we operate in Krabi, your target market
- **No pressure** — we educate customers first, so they're ready to buy
- **Fast payouts** — commission paid within 30 days after client payment

## ❓ FAQ for Partners (Prepare Answers)

**Q: What if the customer doesn't install after I quote?**
A: That's okay, no commission if no installation. Only commission on closed deals.

**Q: What if the customer wants a different system size than I recommend?**
A: You provide the installation, you set the final system size. Our estimate is preliminary. You have the final say.

**Q: How quickly do I need to contact the lead?**
A: Ideally within 24 hours. We'll mark lead as "contacted" in our system. If no contact after 48h, we may reassign.

**Q: What if I disagree with the estimated system size/savings?**
A: You can provide your own quote. Our estimate is for qualification only. We encourage you to give your professional assessment.

**Q: What warranty do you provide on the consultation?**
A: Our consultation is free and non-binding. We don't warranty the numbers; final design is up to your engineering.
