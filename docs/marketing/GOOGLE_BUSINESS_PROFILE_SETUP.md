## 3. Step-by-Step Setup

### Step 1: Go to Google Business Profile

1. Open browser → go to: **google.com/business**
2. Click "Manage now" or "Sign in"
3. Sign in with your Google Account (Gmail)

---

### Step 2: Enter Business Name

**Field:** "Business name"

**What to enter:**
- Use your official business name: **Tawan Power**
- If "Tawan Power Co., Ltd." registered → use that
- Don't add keywords (e.g., "Tawan Power Solar" — save keywords for later)

**What NOT to do:**
- ❌ Don't add location: "Tawan Power Krabi" (address field already has that)
- ❌ Don't add services: "Tawan Power Solar Installation"

---

### Step 3: Business Category

**Field:** "Business category"

**Primary category (choose one):**
- "Solar Panel Installation Service" ← BEST (if you do installations via partners)
- "Solar Energy Consultant" ← good (if only consultation)
- "Home Energy Audit Service" ← alternative
- "Renewable Energy Consultant" ← broader

**Additional categories (add up to 9 more):**
- Solar Energy System Designer
- Electrical Contractor
- Energy Consultant
- Solar Panel Maintenance Service
- Alternative Energy Source

**Why category matters:** Google uses this to understand when to show you in search. If someone searches "solar panel installation", your business shows if you selected that category.

---

### Step 4: Business Address

**Field:** "Business address"

**What to enter:**
- Physical address where you operate from
- If you're home-based: use your home address
- If you have virtual office: use virtual office address (some accept)
- Format: **123/45Ao Nang Road, Mueang Krabi District, Krabi 81000, Thailand**

**Important:**
- Google will mail a postcard with verification code to this address
- Must be able to receive mail at this address
- Can't be P.O. box

**If you don't want to use home address:**
Option 1: Use virtual office (Regus, etc.) — they forward mail
Option 2: Use co-working space address (if they allow)
Option 3: In early stage, use home address (it's okay), later change to office

---

### Step 5: Contact Info

**Phone number:**
- Format: **+66 81 234 5678** (Thai mobile)
- This number appears publicly — use business line if possible
- Can add second number later

**Website:**
- Enter: **https://tawanpower.com**
- Optional but strongly recommended
- If no website yet, you can skip and add later

---

### Step 6: Service Area (If you go to customers)

**Question:** "Do you serve customers at their location?"

**Answer:** **Yes** (if you do site surveys at customer's property)

Then specify:
- City/region: **Krabi** (add Phuket, Phang Nga if serving)
- You can select multiple provinces

**Why important:** This tells Google to show you when people search in those areas, even if your address is in one city.

---

### Step 7: Review & Submit

Check all info is correct → click "Finish"

## 4. Profile Optimization

After creating GBP, you're in **Google Business Profile dashboard**.

### A. Verify Your Business (CRITICAL)

Google won't show your profile publicly until verified.

**Verification methods:**

1. **Postcard (most common)**
   - Google sends postcard with 5-digit verification code
   - Arrives in 5-14 days (to your business address)
   - Enter code in GBP dashboard
   - **Action:** Request postcard immediately after setup

2. **Phone verification** (if eligible)
   - Some businesses get phone option
   - Google calls your business number, provides code
   - Faster (minutes)

3. **Email verification** (rare)
   - If you have GMB before, sometimes email option

**While waiting for postcard:**
- Don't modify business name, address, phone
- You can add photos, services, description
- Profile won't be public until verified

## 5. Getting Your First 10 Reviews

### Why Reviews Matter

- Social proof: 5-star reviews → higher conversion
- Local SEO ranking factor: more reviews → higher in map pack
- Show responsiveness: replying to reviews builds trust

### Rules (Don't Violate)

Google's guidelines:
- ❌ **DON'T** buy reviews
- ❌ **DON'T** offer incentives for reviews (e.g., "get ฿100 off for reviewing")
- ✅ **DO** ask satisfied customers politely
- ✅ **DO** respond to reviews (good and bad)

Violations → suspension of GBP.

---

### Strategy: When to Ask

**Best times to request review:**
1. After successful installation (happy customer)
2. After positive support interaction
3. After consultation (even if not installed yet) — "How was our service?"

**Don't ask:**
- Before work begins (too early, they don't know quality yet)
- If customer was unhappy (they'll give bad review)

## 7. Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue 1: "This business has been suspended"

**Cause:** Usually verification issue or guideline violation.

**Solution:**
1. Check email from Google (why suspended)
2. Fix violation (e.g., removed prohibited keywords)
3. Appeal via GBP dashboard → "Suspend appeal"
4. Wait 2-7 days for review

---

### Issue 2: "Can't verify business" (postcard never arrives)

**Solution:**
1. Check address is exactly correct (match format)
2. Request second postcard (wait 2-3 weeks after first)
3. If still no → use phone verification if available
4. As last resort: create new GBP with corrected address

---

### Issue 3: Duplicate listings

**Symptom:** Two listings for same business (old and new)

**Solution:**
1. Search for your business on Google Maps
2. Find duplicate
3. Claim ownership → merge into one
4. Or request removal of duplicate via GBP support

---

### Issue 4: Reviews disappearing

**Cause:** Google detects fake/incentivized reviews

**Solution:** Never offer incentives for reviews. Only ask organically. If reviews removed, appeal if legitimate.

---

### Issue 5: Wrong business name showing

**Cause:** You entered name incorrectly, or Google merged with another business

**Solution:** Edit in GBP dashboard → change name → verify. If merged incorrectly, contact support.
