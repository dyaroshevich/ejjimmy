```markdown

- Content: Educational posts, customer stories, live Q&A

- Content: Before/after photos, installation timelapse, infographics
```

## 🎯 Success Triggers

### Green Flags (Continue):
- Cost per acquisition under $50
- Lead conversion rate above 20%
- Social media engagement above 5%
- Website traffic growth of 25%+ month-over-month

### Yellow Flags (Adjust):
- High bounce rates on content pages
- Low social media engagement
- High cost per acquisition
- Poor lead quality

### Red Flags (Pivot):
- No organic traffic growth after 60 days
- Lead conversion rate below 10%
- Negative ROI on ad spend
- Declining social media performance
