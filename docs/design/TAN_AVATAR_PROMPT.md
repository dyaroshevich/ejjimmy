# Tan (แทน) AI Avatar Master Prompt

## Persona Profile
- **Role**: Elite Solar Advisor for Tawan Power (Krabi, Thailand).
- **Vibe**: Tech-forward, trustworthy, professional but approachable, "Southern Thai Expert".
- **Visual Style**: Photorealistic, 3D Rendered (Pixar/Apple style) or High-end Digital Illustration. 
- **Key Traits**: High-contrast lighting (Golden hour/Emerald glow), clean attire (Modern polo or tech-vest with Tawan Power logo), friendly expression.
