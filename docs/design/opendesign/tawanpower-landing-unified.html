Knowledge

Answers to everything

How fast is payback in Krabi?

3–5 years at ~5 peak sun hours daily.

What is net-metering?

Sell surplus back to the grid — we file the papers.

Do I need batteries?

Only if you want backup during outages — otherwise grid-tied is cheaper.

Which inverter brands?

Deye, GoodWe and Sigenergy — Tier-1 with local warranty.

Who handles PEA paperwork?

We do — permits and grid connection included.

Hybrid or grid-tied?

Hybrid adds battery backup; grid-tied costs less.

Rainy season — still worth it?

Output drops to 40–60%, still pays off.

What maintenance is needed?

1–2 panel cleanings a year; service plans available.

What warranties apply?

25-year performance, 12-year product warranty.
