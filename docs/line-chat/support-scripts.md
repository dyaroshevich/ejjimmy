## Common Responses

### Pricing Questions
**Q:** "How much does a 10kW system cost?"
**A:** "Approximately ฿500,000-฿700,000 depending on panel brand. We provide exact quote after free site survey."

### Timeline Questions  
**Q:** "How long to install?"
**A:** "Survey: 1-2 days. Permit: 3-7 days. Installation: 2-3 days. Total ~2 weeks."

### Warranty Questions
**Q:** "What about after-sales service?"
**A:** "All systems come with 25-year panel warranty, 10-year inverter warranty. We offer annual cleaning service for ฿5,000/year."

### Installation Process
**Q:** "Do I need permission from authorities?"
**A:** "Yes, we handle all permits through our licensed partners. No hassle for you."

## Escalation Protocol

If customer requests >20% discount:
"I need to check with my manager for special pricing. Let me get back to you within 24 hours."

If customer complains:
"I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. Let me escalate this to our founder who will personally resolve this for you."

VIP clients (hotel chains > 100kW):
"Thank you for your interest. Our founder will personally handle your project. I'm connecting you with them now."
