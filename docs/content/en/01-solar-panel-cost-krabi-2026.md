Most quotes in Krabi land in a narrow band per kilowatt — and the differences between them are rarely the panels. Here is what the numbers look like in 2026, what actually moves them, and the questions that separate a real quote from a brochure.

## Typical installed prices

These are turnkey ranges for a grid-tied residential system: panels, inverter, mounting, DC/AC cabling, protection, installation and commissioning.

| System size | Typical range | Monthly output in Krabi |
|---|---|---|
| 3 kWp | ฿90,000 – ฿130,000 | 330 – 360 kWh |
| 5 kWp | ฿140,000 – ฿190,000 | 550 – 600 kWh |
| 10 kWp | ฿260,000 – ฿380,000 | 1,100 – 1,200 kWh |

Battery storage is priced separately: roughly ฿12,000 – ฿20,000 per kWh installed, so a 10 kWh battery adds ฿120,000 – ฿200,000. It does not pay for itself on tariff savings alone — it buys outage cover and evening independence.

Output assumes a south-facing roof with reasonable tilt and no shade between 9:00 and 15:00. Krabi gets about 4.2–4.6 kWh/m²/day of irradiation, but heat and monsoon cloud cost you 10–15% against a cool-climate benchmark.

## What moves the price

- **Inverter type.** A hybrid inverter (battery-ready) costs 15–30% more than a pure grid-tie unit. Buying hybrid now is cheaper than replacing later, but only if you actually plan a battery.
- **Roof.** Standing-seam metal is the cheapest and fastest. Clay tile needs hooks and careful waterproofing. Flat concrete needs ballasted or penetrated supports. A 3-storey roof with a 40 m cable run adds real money.
- **Electrical work.** An old 5/15 A meter, a undersized consumer unit, or a service that needs upgrading to 3-phase is often the single biggest hidden line item.
- **Shading and layout.** One chimney or a water tank can force a string layout that costs 10% of the array's output — and no amount of premium panel fixes it.

## Does it pay back?

On self-consumption at a residential tariff of roughly ฿4.2–4.5 per unit, a 5 kWp system offsets ฿2,300–2,700 per month if the household is home during the day. That is a 5–7 year payback on the ฿140,000–฿190,000 range, before any export credit.

If nobody is home from 8:00 to 17:00, the same system offsets far less and the payback stretches. That is a load-profile question, not a panel question — and it is the first thing a survey should measure, not guess.

## Five things to check in a quote

1. **Was there a site survey?** A price given over LINE without seeing the roof, the meter and the cable route is a placeholder.
2. **Are panel and inverter models named?** "Tier 1 panels" is not a model number. You cannot compare warranties without it.
3. **Is the DC side protected?** Surge protection, DC isolator, correct cable gauge and a real earth electrode. This is where cheap installs cut first, and where lightning in Krabi finds them.
4. **What is the workmanship warranty, separate from the product warranty?** Panels carry 12–25 years, inverters 5–10, the installer's own work typically 1–5. Get all three in writing.
5. **Who files the utility application?** If the answer is "you do", price that in.
