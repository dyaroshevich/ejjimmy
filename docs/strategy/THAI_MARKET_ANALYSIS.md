## 🏛️ Regulatory Environment

### Permits Required
1. **MEA/PEA interconnection agreement** — net metering
2. **Building permit** ( если самонесущая конструкция)

### Process:
1. Submit application + design drawings
2. Site inspection (1-2 weeks)

4. Installation + inspection
5. Final approval + meter change

## 🇹🇭 Cultural Considerations

### Sales Process
- **Face-to-face meetings** важнее звонков
- **Relationships matter** — spend time building trust
- **Decision makers** — часто семья, не один человек
- **Price sensitivity** — но not always cheapest wins
- **Punctuality** — expected, but Thai time flexible
- **Gift giving** — small gifts appreciated (не被视为 bribes)

### Communication Style
- Indirect — no hard "no"
- Hierarchy — show respect to elders
- Avoid public embarrassment
- Smile, be friendly even if upset

### Negotiation
- Initial quote often above what expected
- Counter-offer expected (10-20% off)
- Give face — don't reject outright, "we discuss"
- Relationship > transaction
