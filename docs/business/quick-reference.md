## 🎯 Lead Status Definitions

- **New:** Just received, not contacted
- **Contacted:** First communication done
- **Survey Scheduled:** Site visit arranged
- **Survey Done:** Engineer visited
- **Quoted:** Price provided
- **Won:** Installation confirmed
- **Lost:** Customer declined
- **Cold:** No response after 72h
