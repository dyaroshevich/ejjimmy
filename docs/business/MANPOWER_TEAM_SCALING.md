## 💼 Role Definitions & Job Descriptions

### 1. Admin (Центральный оператор)

**Salary:** ฿30,000-40,000/mo  
**Schedule:** Mon-Fri 9-18, Sat half-day

**Responsibilities:**
- Monitor new leads (from website, chat, LINE)
- First contact within 1 hour
- Enter lead data into CRM (Pipedrive)
- Schedule consultation calls
- Send initial quote via email
- Update lead status
- Handle simple customer questions

**Skills:**
- Thai + English + Russian (preferred)
- Computer literate (Google Sheets, email)
- Sales-oriented mindset
- Thai nationality preferred (trust factor)

**KPIs:**
- Response time < 1h
- Leads processed/day: 20-30
- Conversion lead → qualified: >30%

---

### 2. Sales Manager (Квалификатор крупных сделок)

**Salary:** ฿40,000-60,000 + commission (2-5% of sale)  
**Schedule:** Flexible, field visits

**Responsibilities:**
- Handle large commercial deals (>฿500k)
- Site visits for assessment
- Detailed proposal preparation
- Negotiation with customers
- Close deals
- Handoff to installation team

**Skills:**
- Solar knowledge (training provided)
- Negotiation skills
- Car owner (for visits)
- Professional appearance

**KPIs:**
- Deals closed/month: 5-10
- Avg deal size: >฿500k
- Commission: ฿20,000-50,000/mo

---

### 3. Installation Lead (Ответственный за качество монтажа)

**Salary:** ฿35,000-50,000 + bonus per install  
**Schedule:** Field + office

**Responsibilities:**
- Coordinate installation schedule
- Supervise installers (quality control)
- Order materials (panels, inverters)
- Site survey before installation
- Customer handover (explanation, manuals)
- Warranty registration
- Photo documentation (before/after)

**Skills:**
- Electrical background (license preferred)
- Management experience
- Customer service

**KPIs:**
- Installations/month: 10-20
- Customer satisfaction > 4.5/5
- Rework rate < 5%

---

### 4. Installer (Монтажник)

**Type:** Contractor (฿5,000-8,000 per installation)  
**Schedule:** On-call, project-based

**Responsibilities:**
- Physical installation of panels, inverters
- Wiring, connection to grid
- Testing, commissioning
- Cleanup after work

**Requirements:**
- Experience with solar installations (preferred)
- Electrical license (advantage)
- Own tools (ladder, drill, etc.)
- Motorbike/car for transport

**Payment model:**
- Per project: ฿5,000 for 5kW system
- Bonus for fast completion (<2 days)
- Penalty for rework

---

### 5. Support Agent (Подиздержка)

**Salary:** ฿20,000-30,000  
**Schedule:** Mon-Fri 9-18, possible weekends on rotation

**Responsibilities:**
- Handle post-installation questions
- Warranty claims processing
- Troubleshooting basic issues
- Coordinate with manufacturers (RMA)

**Skills:**
- Technical aptitude

**KPIs:**
- Response time < 4h
- Resolution rate first contact >70%

## 🔄 Workflow Processes

### Lead Processing Pipeline

```
1. Lead enters (website/chat/LINE)
   ↓
2. Admin notified (Web Push + Telegram)
   ↓
3. Admin contacts within 1 hour (LINE/Phone)
   ↓
4. Basic qualification (budget, timeline, location)
   ↓
5. Send initial quote (PDF)
   ↓
6. If large (>฿500k) → Sales Manager
   ↓
7. Site visit scheduling
   ↓
8. Detailed quote + contract
   ↓
9. Deposit payment (30%)
   ↓
10. Installation scheduling
   ↓
11. Installation (2-3 days)
   ↓
12. Final payment + warranty handover
   ↓
13. Support post-install (30 days)
```

---

### Installation Process

```
Day 1:
├── Site arrival, safety briefing
├── Install racking/mounting
├── Mount panels
└── Electrical rough-in

Day 2:
├── Inverter installation
├── Wiring connections
├── Grid connection permit
└── Testing

Day 3:
├── Final inspection
├── Commissioning
├── Customer training
└── Documentation/photos
```

**Checklist for installers:**
- [ ] PPE (safety gear)
- [ ] Tools checklist
- [ ] Materials confirm (panel SN, inverter SN)
- [ ] Permit obtained
- [ ] Grounding tested
- [ ] Production monitoring setup
- [ ] Customer signed off

## 🇹🇭 Thai Labor Considerations

### Employment Types
1. **Full-time (บันทึกข้อความ/คำสั่ง kerja)** — benefits, severance, social security
2. **Contract ( temporary)** — no benefits, easier to terminate
3. **Freelance/Contractor** — outside company, no payroll taxes

### Required Benefits (full-time)
- Social Security (在留カード): employer 5%, employee 5%
- Workman's Compensation: ~0.2-1% payroll
- Severance: 先Serve notice или pay in lieu:
  - <120 days: 7 days notice
  - 120-365 days: 30 days
  - 1-3 years: 60 days
  - 3-6 years: 90 days
  - 6-10 years: 180 days
  - 10+ years: 300 days

### Minimum Wage (Thailand 2026)
- Bangkok: ฿354-379/day (чисто ฿7,500-8,000/mo)
- Provinces: ฿323-336/day

**Our pay rates:** ~฿20,000-60,000/mo → competitive

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Installer quality varies | Certification program, checklist, penalties for rework |
| Admin burns out (1 person) | Hire assistant immediately after 50 leads/mo |

| Employee turnover (Thailand tight labor market) | Competitive pay, bonuses, good culture |
| Training takes longer | Have 2-week onboarding plan, mentor system |
| Installation delays due to weather | Buffer in schedule, weather insurance |
