## 📋 Escalation Decision Tree

### Level 1: Support Staff (First Contact)
**Handles:** Standard inquiries, initial lead contact, survey scheduling

**When to escalate:** Skip to Level 2 if:
- Customer requests >15% discount
- Technical impossibility from AI
- VIP client (hotel >100kW)
- Multiple failed contact attempts

### Level 2: Engineer/Support Lead
**Handles:** Site surveys, technical consultations, standard complaints

**When to escalate:** Skip to Level 3 if:
- Customer requests >20% discount
- Serious service failure
- Legal threats
- System outage >30 minutes

### Level 3: Founder
**Handles:** All escalations, special requests, crisis management

## 🎯 Specific Escalation Scenarios

### ⚠️ High Priority (Immediate Escalation to Founder)

| Situation | Response Time | Action |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| **System Outage** > 1 hour | Within 15 min | 1. Inform all active users<br>2. Check API status<br>3. Communicate timeline |
| **Legal Threat** | Within 30 min | 1. Do not admit fault<br>2. Document everything<br>3. Lawyer consultation |
| **VIP Client** (>100kW) | Within 1 hour | 1. Personal handling<br>2. Custom pricing<br>3. Direct founder contact |
| **Service Failure** | Within 1 hour | 1. Immediate apology<br>2. Compensation offered<br>3. Root cause fix |

### ⚠️ Medium Priority (Escalate to Engineer Lead)

| Situation | Response Time | Action |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| **Price Negotiation** >20% | Within 2 hours | 1. Explain standard pricing<br>2. Offer alternatives<br>3. If needed → Founder |
| **Technical Issue** | Within 4 hours | 1. Verify AI calculations<br>2. Engineer to recalculate<br>3. Update system if needed |
| **Multiple Contact Attempts** | Within 24 hours | 1. New strategy<br>2. Different team member<br>3. Consider "cold" status |

### ⚠️ Low Priority (Handle at Support Level)

| Situation | Response Time | Action |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| **Standard Inquiry** | Within 4 hours | 1. Use support scripts<br>2. Follow standard process<br>3. Document response |
| **Information Request** | Within 8 hours | 1. Provide accurate info<br>2. Follow up if needed<br>3. Add to knowledge base |
| **Follow-up Reminder** | Within 24 hours | 1. Send reminder message<br>2. Update status<br>3. Schedule next contact |

## 📊 Escalation Metrics

Track monthly:
- **Escalation Rate:** % of cases requiring escalation
- **Resolution Time:** Average time to resolve escalated cases
- **Re-escalation:** Cases that need multiple escalations
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Post-resolution feedback

## 🚀 De-escalation Checklist

Before escalating, verify:
- [ ] Standard process followed completely
- [ ] Customer communication documented
- [ ] Alternative solutions offered
- [ ] No miscommunication occurred
- [ ] Issue cannot be resolved at current level
